New Publication in Lab on a Chip

Our latest research on advanced microfluidic systems for MiMic applications has been published in the prestigious Lab on a Chip journal. The paper presents novel design principles for creating more physiologically relevant tissue models.

Award
Jan 10, 2024

Dr. Bianchi Wins Best Poster Award at MicroTAS 2024

Congratulations to Dr. Maria Bianchi for receiving the Best Poster Award at the 28th International Conference on Miniaturized Systems for Chemistry and Life Sciences. Her work on heart MiMic biosensors was recognized for its innovation and impact.

Event
Jan 5, 2024

Workshop: Microfluidic Device Fabrication

Join us for a hands-on workshop on microfluidic device fabrication and characterization. The two-day event will cover soft lithography, PDMS molding, and device validation techniques. Registration now open for graduate students and researchers.

News
Dec 20, 2023

Lab Featured in Nature Reviews Bioengineering

Our research on multi-organ platforms has been highlighted in a comprehensive review article in Nature Reviews Bioengineering. The article discusses the future of microphysiological systems in drug development.

Award

New ERC Starting Grant Awarded

Prof. Rossi has been awarded a prestigious ERC Starting Grant totaling €1.5M for the project 'Advanced MiMic Systems with Multi-Modal Sensing'. The five-year project will push the boundaries of microfluidic biosensor integration.

News
Dec 1, 2023

International Collaboration Announced with MIT

We are excited to announce a new research collaboration with Professor Linda Griffith's laboratory at MIT. The partnership will focus on developing multi-MiMic systems for studying liver-immune system interactions.

Alessandro Ferrari successfully defended his PhD thesis titled 'Gut MiMic Systems with Integrated Microbiome for Host-Microbe Interaction Studies'. Congratulations Dr. Ferrari on this outstanding achievement!
