Our Research

Pioneering innovations in microfluidic technologies and MiMic systems to advance biomedical research and drug discovery.

Research Focus

Our laboratory conducts cutting-edge research at the intersection of bioengineering, microfabrication, and cell biology. We develop innovative MiMic platforms that replicate human physiology for applications in drug testing, disease modeling, and personalized medicine.

Cardiac Tissue Modeling

Development of heart MiMic systems with integrated electrical and mechanical sensors for studying cardiac physiology, drug cardiotoxicity, and disease mechanisms. Our platforms incorporate human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes in 3D microenvironments.

Heart MiMicCardiologyDrug Testing
Learn more

Liver Toxicity Platforms

Advanced liver MiMic models combining hepatocytes, endothelial cells, and Kupffer cells to predict drug-induced liver injury. Our systems enable long-term culture and metabolic profiling for pharmaceutical applications.

Liver MiMicToxicologyDrug Metabolism
Learn more

Gut Microbiome Systems

Microfluidic platforms that co-culture intestinal epithelium with complex microbial communities under physiological oxygen gradients. Applications include studying inflammatory bowel disease and host-microbe interactions.

Gut MiMicMicrobiomeIBD
Learn more

Lung Models for Infection Studies

Air-liquid interface lung MiMic systems that replicate breathing mechanics and enable infection studies with respiratory pathogens. Relevant for COVID-19 research and inhalation toxicology.

Lung MiMicRespiratoryInfection
Learn more

Vascularized Tissue Engineering

Creating perfusable vascular networks in MiMic devices using 3D bioprinting and microfabrication. Essential for improving nutrient delivery and tissue viability in complex organ models.

Vascularization3D BioprintingTissue Engineering
Learn more

Biosensor Integration

Development of electrochemical and optical biosensors for real-time monitoring of tissue function, metabolism, and barrier integrity in MiMic systems. Non-invasive and label-free detection methods.

BiosensorsReal-time MonitoringElectrochemistry
Learn more

Tumor Microenvironment Models

Cancer MiMic platforms that recapitulate the tumor microenvironment including immune cells, vasculature, and extracellular matrix. Used for studying metastasis and immunotherapy responses.

CancerTumor ModelsImmunotherapy
Learn more

Blood-Brain Barrier Platforms

Microfluidic models of the blood-brain barrier for studying neurodegenerative diseases and evaluating drug permeability. Incorporates brain endothelial cells, pericytes, and astrocytes.

Multi-Organ Platforms

Body MiMic systems connecting multiple organ modules to study inter-organ communication, systemic drug effects, and pharmacokinetics. Enables more predictive preclinical testing.

Research Facilities

Microfabrication Lab

State-of-the-art cleanroom facilities equipped with photolithography, soft lithography, and 3D printing capabilities for device fabrication.

Cell Culture Facilities

Dedicated cell culture rooms with incubators, biosafety cabinets, and equipment for maintaining various cell lines and primary cultures.

Imaging Suite

Advanced microscopy systems including confocal, fluorescence, and live-cell imaging platforms for real-time monitoring.

Analytical Laboratory

Comprehensive analytical equipment for biochemical assays, molecular biology, and characterization of biological samples.
