Collaborations

Building bridges across continents to advance MiMic technology through international partnerships and collaborative research.

Global Research Network

Our lab collaborates with leading research institutions, universities, and industry partners worldwide. These partnerships enable knowledge exchange, resource sharing, and joint research initiatives that drive innovation in microfluidics and MiMic technologies.

Academic Partners

Massachusetts Institute of Technology

Cambridge, MA, USA

Collaboration on microfluidic device design and multi-MiMic platforms with Prof. Linda Griffith's lab.

ETH Zurich

Zurich, Switzerland

Joint research on biosensor integration and real-time monitoring in MiMic systems.

University of Cambridge

Cambridge, UK

Partnership on iPSC-derived tissue models and cardiac electrophysiology studies.

Harvard Medical School

Boston, MA, USA

Collaborative projects on disease modeling and drug discovery using MiMic technology.

Imperial College London

London, UK

Joint research on lung MiMic models for respiratory disease and infection studies.

National University of Singapore

Singapore

Collaboration on liver-on-chip platforms and drug metabolism studies.
