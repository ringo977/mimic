Collaborations

Building bridges across institutions, countries, and disciplines to advance organ-on-chip technology through collaborative research.

Our Research Network

The MiMic Lab collaborates with leading universities, research hospitals, and industry partners across Europe and beyond. Through EU-funded projects like PHOENIX, BuonMarrow, and EvOoC, as well as bilateral partnerships, we combine microfluidic engineering with biology, medicine, and pharmaceutical science to develop the next generation of organ-on-chip platforms.

Research Institutes & Hospitals

Clinical and research institutions driving translational impact in biomedicine.

HRI

Humanitas Research Hospital

Rozzano (Milan), Italy

Long-standing collaboration on liver and cardiac disease modeling. Joint projects with Prof. Ana Lleo De Nalda on cholangiocarcinoma-on-chip, and with Prof. Elisa Di Pasquale on cardiac models within the Horizon Europe PHOENIX project.

Visit website
CNR

CNR @ San Raffaele Hospital

Milan, Italy

Collaboration with Dr. Vania Broccoli at the Institute of Neuroscience (CNR) within San Raffaele Hospital, focused on neuromuscular modeling and neurodegenerative disease research using organ-on-chip platforms.

Visit website
IEO

IEO — European Institute of Oncology

Milan, Italy

Key partnership on immuno-oncology and tumor microenvironment modeling. Collaboration with Prof. Luigi Nezi on the gut-on-chip platform published in Nature Biomedical Engineering, and with Prof. Luisa Lanfrancone on tumor-on-chip models.

Industry Partners

Companies and spin-offs transforming organ-on-chip research into real-world applications.

BX

BiomimX

Organ-on-Chip Technology

Spin-off company of Politecnico di Milano co-founded by MiMic Lab. BiomimX develops and commercializes the patented uBeat technology — mechanically active organ-on-chip platforms for drug discovery and preclinical testing.

Visit website
CF

Chiesi Farmaceutici

Pharmaceuticals

Collaboration on lung-on-chip models for the study of chronic respiratory diseases including COPD and asthma, combining Chiesi's pharmaceutical expertise with MiMic Lab's microfluidic platform technologies.

Visit website
MN

Micronit

Microfluidics

Dutch leader in the development of microfluidic devices and partner in the Horizon Europe PHOENIX project. Micronit provides cutting-edge microfabrication capabilities for next-generation organ-on-chip platforms.

Visit website
MCS

Multi Channel Systems (MCS)

Electrophysiology

World leader in electrophysiology recording systems and partner in the Horizon Europe PHOENIX project. MCS provides advanced multi-electrode array technology for real-time functional readouts in organ-on-chip models.
