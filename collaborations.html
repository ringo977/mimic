Collaborations

Building bridges across continents to advance organ-on-chip technology through international partnerships and collaborative research.

Global Research Network

Our lab collaborates with leading research institutions, universities, and industry partners worldwide. These partnerships enable knowledge exchange, resource sharing, and joint research initiatives that drive innovation in microfluidics and organ-on-chip technologies.

Academic Partners

Massachusetts Institute of Technology

Cambridge, MA, USA

Collaboration on microfluidic device design and multi-organ platforms with Prof. Linda Griffith's lab.

ETH Zurich

Zurich, Switzerland

Joint research on biosensor integration and real-time monitoring in organ-on-chip systems.

University of Cambridge

Cambridge, UK

Partnership on iPSC-derived tissue models and cardiac electrophysiology studies.

Harvard Medical School

Boston, MA, USA

Collaborative projects on disease modeling and drug discovery using organ-on-chip technology.

Imperial College London

London, UK

Joint research on lung-on-chip models for respiratory disease and infection studies.

National University of Singapore

Singapore

Collaboration on liver-on-chip platforms and drug metabolism studies.

Industry Partners

Emulate Bio

Biotechnology

Partnership on commercialization of organ-on-chip platforms and validation studies.

Roche Pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceuticals

Collaborative drug testing and toxicity screening using our organ-on-chip models.

AstraZeneca

Pharmaceuticals

Joint projects on cardiac safety testing and liver toxicity assessment platforms.

TissUse GmbH

Biotechnology

Technology exchange on multi-organ chips and microfluidic pump systems.

Pfizer

Pharmaceuticals

Research partnership on organ-on-chip models for early-stage drug development.

Major Collaborative Projects

ERC Starting Grant: Advanced Organ-on-Chip Systems

2023-2028

Five-year project funded by the European Research Council to develop next-generation organ-on-chip platforms with integrated multi-modal sensing capabilities.

Partners:ETH ZurichUniversity of Cambridge

Horizon Europe: Multi-Organ Disease Modeling

2022-2026

International consortium developing interconnected organ-on-chip systems for studying systemic diseases and drug effects.

Partners:MITHarvard Medical SchoolImperial College London

Industry Partnership: Cardiac Safety Testing Platform

2023-2025

Collaborative project with pharmaceutical companies to establish standardized heart-on-chip assays for drug cardiotoxicity screening.

Partners:RocheAstraZenecaEmulate Bio

NIH NCATS Tissue Chip Program

2021-2025

US-EU collaboration on developing microphysiological systems for translational research and drug development.

Partners:MITTissUse GmbHNational University of Singapore
