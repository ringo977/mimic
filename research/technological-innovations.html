Biosensors & Real-Time Monitoring

We develop electrochemical and optical biosensors for non-invasive, real-time monitoring of tissue function, metabolism, and barrier integrity within organ-on-chip systems. Our label-free detection methods enable continuous data acquisition without disrupting cell cultures, providing rich longitudinal datasets for drug response and disease progression studies.
