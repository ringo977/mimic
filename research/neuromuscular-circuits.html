Neuromuscular Circuits

Organ-on-chip models of the neuromuscular junction integrating motor neurons, skeletal muscle cells, and supporting tissues to investigate neuromuscular disorders and drug responses.

Neuromuscular Junction on Chip

We develop compartmentalized microfluidic platforms that recreate the neuromuscular junction by co-culturing motor neurons and skeletal muscle fibers in separate but fluidically connected chambers. Axonal growth through microchannels enables synapse formation and functional neuromuscular coupling, providing a powerful tool for studying ALS, myasthenia gravis, and neurotoxicity.
